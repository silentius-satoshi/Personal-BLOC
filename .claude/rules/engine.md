# Rules — the engine, the support policy, the faces, THE MOVE and crash day

Read first, for what you touch: `docs/claude/engines.md`, `docs/claude/almanac.md`, `docs/claude/crash.md`, `docs/claude/test-suite.md` (its first 🔴 notes, at the top).

Each rule's full text — its mechanism, measures and history — is in `docs/claude/rules-in-full.md` § `engine.md`.
